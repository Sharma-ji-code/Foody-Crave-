Our Story

A café born from passion

Foody Crave was born in 2026 with one simple dream — to create a place in Cuttack where food is pure, the ambience is warm, and every visit feels like a celebration.

Nestled beside JP Narayan Park in CDA Sector 6, we are Cuttack's newest 100% pure vegetarian café and celebration venue. From a morning espresso to a grand anniversary dinner in our private suite, every moment here is crafted with love and intention.

We believe that vegetarian food can be bold, exciting, and world-class. Our kitchen brings together recipes from across India and around the world — all plant-based, all fresh, all delicious.
