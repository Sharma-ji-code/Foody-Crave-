Our Story

A café that feels like home

Foody Crave is Cuttack's newest pure vegetarian café and celebration destination — nestled beside JP Narayan Park in CDA Sector 6. From a morning espresso to a grand anniversary dinner, every moment here is crafted with love.

Our private rooms, theater suite, and bespoke celebration packages make every occasion unforgettable.
